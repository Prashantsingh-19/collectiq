The model never does arithmetic

Balances, ages and scores come from code and go to the model as a fact sheet. A reminder with the wrong amount damages a customer relationship, so any draft whose numbers don't match the fact sheet cannot be sent.

Rank by risk, not by size

A large bill that is five days late matters less than a medium one that is eighty days late from a customer who often pays late. The score weighs amount 45%, days overdue 35% and past late-payment rate 20%.

Escalation follows fixed rules

1–15 days: friendly reminder. 16–45: firm reminder. 46–75: the owner gets a call task. Over 75: pause new credit. Rules decide the step; the model only writes the message.

The owner stays in control

In Indian B2B trade, relationships carry the business. Nothing reaches a customer until the owner approves it, and the tone stays polite at every stage.
